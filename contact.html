Who We Are

Aware Together is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to preventing the
spread of medical misinformation and promoting health and wellness awareness.

We're organizing a large-scale health and wellness event marketed to students across Frisco, Prosper, McKinney, Plano, and surrounding areas. Our goal is to create an engaging, informative space where students, parents, educators, and community members can learn about maintaining healthy habits, improving overall wellness, and prioritizing mindfulness as we head into a new school year.

Our journey began with a shared passion for promoting health literacy, combating medical misinformation, and advocating for holistic healthcare practices. We recognized that despite the vast array of information available today, essential health topics were often overshadowed, misunderstood, or neglected — and we set out to bridge that gap.

We believe everyone deserves access to reliable, evidence-based health information, regardless of background or resources. Through continuous research, collaboration with healthcare professionals, and community outreach, Aware Together is working to shed light on underappreciated health issues and empower our community to make confident, informed healthcare decisions. Join us as we work to build a healthier, more informed world — one where knowledge is power and well-being is within reach for all.

Encouraging habits that support a healthier, more mindful lifestyle.

Open conversation addressing common questions families have about health and wellness.

Hosted at GracePoint Church in McKinney, TX on March 25th, 2023.
